Should educated votes count more?

There's a good candidate and a bad candidate. 1,001 voters each guess who's better, nudged by the news they follow, and plenty of them guess wrong. We count every election twice: once with every vote equal, once with educated votes counting extra. Which way picks the good candidate more often?

A toy model, not a forecast. Everyone has the same personal judgement noise except that educated voters are a bit sharper; what differs is which news they hear. Africa is not included: Afrobarometer did not ask the factual questions needed to measure shared mistakes, which is the one number that decides the result. Code and the check against the research model: github.com/yaqzans/who-should-count-more.
