A voting experiment

Who Should Count More?

Every dot is a voter, linked to the news sources they follow. Two candidates stand; one of them is genuinely better. Hold an election: each source leans one way, the lean spreads to the people who listen to it, and the count runs twice, once with every vote equal and once with educated votes counting more. Then hold a thousand and see which system picks the better candidate more often.
